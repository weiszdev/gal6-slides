HOW IT WORKS

Open the presenter and the two view windows in the same browser (same profile). Advance in the presenter (arrow keys, Space, a clicker, or click the slide). The wide and vertical windows follow instantly.

Share the wide window to StreamYard as a screen share in landscape, and the vertical window as a second screen share in portrait. In OBS, add wide.html as a 1920×1080 browser source and vert.html as a 1080×1920 browser source. See README.md.

Start on a given slide: presenter.html?slide=7. Black screen: press B. Green-screen camera slot for OBS keying: wide.html?key=1, vert.html?key=1.
